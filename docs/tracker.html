Where we are

Infrastructure is complete and reviewed:
  • exact simulator
  • PPO trainer (~240M samples/h)
  • self-play league with PFSP and mirror self-play
  • single-file exporter, about 1 ms callbacks against the 30 ms limit
  • paired-seed evaluation harness
  • automatic held-out evaluation
The first review found two real reward bugs, now fixed: death penalties disappeared after 200 deaths, and progress shaping could be farmed by dying. Shaping is now potential-based. M2 restarted as run m2b (learning to score against idle opponents). In progress: review round 2 plus Phase 2; five new scripted training opponents; a replay viewer.
