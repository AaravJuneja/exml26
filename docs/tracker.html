Where we are

The policy is learning to score. Run m2e reached 3.2 goals per match against idle opponents after 38M samples (about 10 minutes of training), with ~0.6 wall deaths per player. On held-out maps it beats idle 71% of the time; baseline is 0% so far, since it hasn't trained against real opponents yet (that's M3).

Since the last update:
  • Review round 2 found and we fixed: a reward leak of the teammate carrier's position, critic drift (fixed with PopArt), and normalisation drift.
  • Your ramp fix is applied, and the engine is byte-identical to game-env.
  • A discounting side effect in the shaping (a per-tick bonus for idling far from the flag) was diagnosed and fixed; it had stalled two runs.
  • Added: a long-horizon win-objective option, a replay viewer (docs/viewer.html), and an exporter latency verdict.
