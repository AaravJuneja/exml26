Where we are

Strategy reset after independent surveys. Both reviewers found that plain RL has plateaued against relay and balanced: about 15-20% wins, with goal difference improving only ~0.3 per 100M samples. The gap is behaviour, not action expressivity: relay rounded to our action bins, with sonar disabled, still beats us ~70%. We hit walls 1-1.7 times per player per match; relay's whole team averages ~0.04.

Now running:
  • k1 (kickstarting): PPO plus an imitation loss toward a shadow relay teacher's actions on our own states (DAgger-style), fading over 150M samples. So far wall deaths are down to 0.83 per player; goals dipped mid-transition.
  • m5b: the unchanged control.
  • Plan B in development: the network picks heading and speed, and a fixed physics controller drives the sphere (the probe found cloning works much better there).
Methodology fixes: one or two focused jobs instead of four (throughput per job back up); paired A/B tool (128 games per opponent with CIs); re-pickup delay tracked. The league's frozen opponents now play greedy, like the deployed model. Repo: front page index.html + model gallery/viewer.
