Where we are

New main release: plan B (teams/exml_rl), 72% wins on 640 held-out games, up from 54% for the pure-RL release. It beats all provided bots 100% (goal diff about +11), steady 95%, rusher 73%, chaos 72%, camper 66%, guardian 63%, relay 28%, balanced 25%. Wall deaths are ~0.1 per player, vs ~1.3 before. Latency is p99 0.37 ms on a quiet machine (max 13.7 ms with 6 CPU-burning processes); audit and parity pass.

Architecture: a learned desired-velocity policy (PPO, heading/speed/jump each tick) with a deterministic physics actuator, kickstarted from a scripted teacher, then RL. Kept: the pure end-to-end RL release as teams/exml_rl_pure. In progress: distillation of plan B into a pure end-to-end network (a separate run), so we also have a fully compliant model at plan-B strength. b1 keeps training on the training machine.
