Where we are

Breakthrough: plan B. The network now chooses a desired heading and speed each tick, and a fixed physics controller (inlined in model.py) turns that into throttle and turn. Trained from scratch with kickstarting from the relay bot, run b1 matched our best old-action-space model in about 45 minutes (58M samples). Our older models took 15+ hours:
  • baseline 99% (goal diff +9.3)
  • relay 15%, balanced 14%
  • rusher 41%
  • wall deaths 0.07 per player, vs ~1.2 before
  • 41% agreement with the teacher, vs 15% in the old action space
The imitation weight is still 0.8 and fading, so there's room to climb. All 9 training workers are now on b1.

Failed and parked: kickstarting in the old throttle/turn-bin action space (k1, k2), the focused relay curriculum (m5b), and the generalist league (g1, flat). The release (teams/exml_rl, m4c u210) is still the best submitted model until b1 beats it on the paired 128-game test.
