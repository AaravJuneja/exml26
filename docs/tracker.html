Where we are

M3→M4 transition. The policy beats baseline about 90% and the mid-strength bots 15-35%, but relay and balanced are still about 5-15%.

A strategy review identified coordination and control as the gap: in the relay replay only 58% of our pickups became captures, against 82% for relay. Changes made:
  • Features v4: which teammate carries, teammate respawn countdowns, enemies near our home, closing speed on our stolen flag, and how long the enemy flag has sat at home
  • symmetric fine throttle bins
  • a defence reward potential, which beat the control slightly vs relay
  • a focused curriculum: relay and balanced 25% each, plus a league pool of the run's own past snapshots
Running an A/B on exploration and credit settings (m4a: entropy 0.003, λ 0.95 vs m4b: entropy 0.0005, λ 0.99). Both warm-start from the previous best via function-preserving surgery (v3→v4 migration, Net2Wider 256×256).
