Where we are

M3: past baseline, plateaued against the two strongest bots. The policy now beats baseline on 84-97% of held-out maps, and guardian/camper/rusher on 15-28%. Against relay and balanced (~11 goals per match) it has been flat at about 5-15% for ~200M samples.

Findings:
  • Greedy decoding is far stronger than sampling (vs relay 28% vs 6% on fresh seeds), so model.py will use greedy.
  • A/B test: a higher learning rate (1e-3) made things worse, so step size isn't the limit.
Now testing network capacity: m3e-c widens the actor to 256x256 with function-preserving Net2Wider surgery, against control m3e-a. Next candidate: a defence reward potential (chase our stolen flag), aimed at relay's slow 'waiter' that camps on our flag.
