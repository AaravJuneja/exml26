Where we are

M3 in progress, run m3d: held-out u300 beats baseline 94%, camper 22%, guardian 16%, rusher 16%; relay 6%, balanced 3%. Against relay on seed 1003 we improved from 2-19 to 7-14; our average speed rose from about 6 to about 8 u/s (relay averages about 11). Work started on optimizing the rollout hot path (exactness-tested) for more training throughput. The clear-ground features (v2) lifted held-out wins vs baseline from 44% to 78% (m3c, snapshot u50). The strong scripted bots (relay, balanced, ~11 goals per match) are still mostly unbeaten, at 3-9%.

The review of the new features found real false-safety issues, now fixed:
  • rays could slip diagonally past blocked cells; they now use exact grid traversal
  • airborne or ramp positions were counted as clear ground; now only grounded, ground-level positions count
  • nearby bumps didn't block their own cells; they now do
Throughput:
  • the learner moved to the GPU, ~10× faster per step
  • rollouts are now pipelined with updates
  • result: 17k → ~27k ticks/s, about 290M learner samples per hour
