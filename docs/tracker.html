Where we are

Steady progress. The best line (m4c: v4 features, 256×256, entropy 0.0001, λ 0.995, focused league curriculum) now beats, on held-out maps:
  • baseline 94-96%
  • guardian / rusher / camper ~48-56%, up from 15-35% earlier
  • relay ~6-15% and balanced ~8-17%, with goal difference narrowing to about -3
The review of the v5 migration found real risks, now fixed: policy files are never auto-migrated (strict loading), and lossy migrations are refused. v5 memory features (m5a) tied or slightly beat v4, so m5a is now the mainline. Replay vs relay: the newest v4 snapshot won 8-5 on the map it previously lost 2-19 and 7-14. The remaining gap is how fast we re-take the flag after it resets: a median of 195 ticks for us vs 51 for relay. A/B now: m5b adds a support potential (rewards waiting near the enemy flag while a teammate carries). First real submission released: teams/exml_rl/model.py (m4c u210). Audit and deployed-file parity PASS. Latency PASS on a quiet machine: p99 0.29 ms, max 3.1 ms (with 6 CPU-burning processes: p99 1.4 ms, max 14 ms; the limit is 30 ms). Held-out: 54% wins overall vs 10 bots (table below). Up from 41% for the first candidate.
